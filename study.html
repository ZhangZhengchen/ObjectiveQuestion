What four experts will do

Each expert independently reviews all thirty shuffled, method-anonymous items: 30 reviews per expert, 120 reviews in total, with four 1–5 ratings per item (480 dimension ratings). Experts see the common teacher-approved item focus, needed course evidence, question, answer and explanation. They do not see condition labels, generation traces or model ratings.

Experts judge the final item, not every authoring step. A teacher prepares and freezes the ten themes and source-backed references before generation. Before main rating, use a few separate practice items to clarify the rubric without changing the frozen experimental items. Experts record ambiguities, incorrect keys or shortcuts in comments; these comments do not trigger selective regeneration of study items.

Three conditions, thirty items

Ten frozen AI-course themes × three conditions × one output. A: bounded course content directly to item. B: the same plus selected concepts. C: the same plus the relation, scenario and approved focus. Use the same generator, settings, format policy and output limit; retain first outputs, record failures and avoid quality-driven retries. Freeze these settings before execution. Repeated-generation stability is outside this study.

This compares a structured preparation package, not an isolated causal effect of adding a relation sentence. Record format distributions and preparation effort.

How results will be reported

Average experts within each item, then weight the ten themes equally within each condition. There is one item per theme and condition. Report aspect means, medians, rating coverage and ordinal Krippendorff’s alpha. Missing ratings are not zero. If coverage permits, compare theme blocks using Friedman tests, followed where warranted by paired Wilcoxon tests with Holm adjustment and an effect size. Four ratings of the same item are not independent experimental samples.

Qualitative flags include recall-only, obvious answer, weak distractor, lexical cue, irrelevant scenario and lost relation. Two reviewers independently code these issues; report initial agreement before reconciliation. LLM ratings, if later collected, remain supplementary and separate.
