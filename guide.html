OBJECTIVE QUESTION STUDYRating Guide
Open review

Three aspects. Five rating levels.

Evaluate final questions using the stated focus, question, reference answer and response rules. The standards are a simplified, literature-informed adaptation, not a previously validated three-dimension scale. Do not rate an original open-ended task, generation method or perceived student difficulty.

Target Alignment

Does the question assess the stated knowledge or judgment?

1The intended target is absent.
2Only a tangential aspect of the target is assessed.
3The target is partly assessed, with material scope drift.
4The target is directly assessed, with a minor scope issue.
5The question precisely assesses the stated target.

Answer and Scoring Validity

Are the reference answer and scoring rules defensible under the stated conditions?

1The key is incorrect or scoring is contradictory.
2Major answer or scoring problems remain.
3The key is plausible, but an important interpretation or scoring boundary needs repair.
4The answer and rules are defensible, with minor clarification needed.
5All accepted and rejected responses are defensible; scoring is clear and consistent.

Question Design Quality

Is the question clearly designed, without obvious clues or implausible alternatives that bypass the target?

1Wording or answer cues defeat the intended question.
2Major clues, implausible alternatives or unclear instructions require redesign.
3The question is usable, but noticeable design issues need revision.
4The question is clear and well designed, with only minor issues.
5Wording, alternatives and response instructions work well; no material shortcut is evident.

How ratings will be summarized

Four experts use the same three criteria. For each course and each criterion, first average the four ratings of each question, then average those question means with equal weight. Report one decimal place in the paper and disclose the number of questions and reviewers.

Question mean = (E1 + E2 + E3 + E4) / 4
Course mean = sum of complete question means / number of complete questions

Missing scores are never replaced with zero. Incomplete four-reviewer coverage is flagged; final paper cells remain blank until coverage is complete. No overall score is formed by adding the three criteria.

Ordinal Krippendorff’s alpha describes reviewer agreement. Detailed distributions and 95% intervals from item-level bootstrap resampling are supplementary. A coefficient can be undefined when there is no score variation; this is reported explicitly.

Comments about incorrect answers, scoring ambiguity or unintended clues are inspected separately. A high mean in other aspects does not cancel an answer-validity concern.

Where these standards come from

A Brief Introduction to Evidence-Centered Design (Mislevy, Almond & Lukas, 2003)
Alignment of intended claims, observable evidence and tasks.

A Review of Multiple-Choice Item-Writing Guidelines (Haladyna, Downing & Rodriguez, 2002)
Content/behavior alignment, defensible answers, clear directions, plausible alternatives and avoidance of clues.

Threats to the Validity of Locally Developed Multiple-Choice Tests (Downing, 2002)
Construct underrepresentation and construct-irrelevant cues; not an item-difficulty scale.

The adaptation extends single-choice principles to multiple-select, matching, ordering, tied ranking and judgment matrices. Legitimate multiple responses and ties must be retained.

Saving and returning your review

Choose the reviewer ID assigned to you. Work is saved locally in the current browser when possible. Download the completed JSON file and send it to the study coordinator through your usual channel. The website does not upload scores or collect names or email addresses.

You can download a draft and restore it later. The export records the exact question and rubric version. Do not inspect other reviewers’ ratings until your own ratings are complete.
