Execution and review

New-study generation has not run. Model, reasoning setting and output-token limit must be frozen before execution and recorded with the actual request/response logs. There are no new model scores or completed expert ratings to disclose.

Four experts independently rate alignment, answer validity, item quality and intended cognitive demand. The expert rubric and aggregation plan are separate from generation prompts. Teacher source/target confirmation is an authoring decision. The workbench does not run automatic numerical scoring or regeneration loops.

No model-based scoring prompt is used by the current offline workflow. Earlier diagnostic-review instructions are included below; they are not the expert-rating rubric.
